# Getting started with QuizBox (public site)

Audience: a new visitor. Build: `www.quizbox.io`, 2026-10-21.

## What you can see without an account

* **Home** (`/`): the tagline "Learn. Practise. Compete.", who QuizBox is for, subjects, and the way in. Screenshot: `screenshots/public-home-desktop-1366.png`.
* **Challenges** (`/challenges`) and **Competitions** (`/competitions`): lists of open quizzes anyone can join. When none are open the page says "No open challenges right now" and invites you to sign in or create an account.
* **Pricing** (`/pricing`): plans for learners, teachers and schools, sponsors and companies. At the time of capture the cards show "Price to be announced" (see defect QB-AUD-002). Screenshots: `screenshots/public-pricing-desktop-1366.png` (desktop), `screenshots/public-pricing-mobile-390.png` (phone).
* **Contact, Privacy, Terms**.

## Joining

1. Select **Get started** (top right) to create an account, or **Sign in** if you have one.
2. Choose your role (learner, teacher, sponsor) and complete onboarding. Children under 13 use accounts created by a teacher; they cannot pay online.
3. After sign-in the header on the public pages changes to **Open my workspace** and takes you to your home page.

## Joining a challenge or competition

Open a quiz from the list, select **Play now** (challenge) or **Join the competition**. You need a free account; if you are not signed in you are taken to sign in and come straight back. Everyone gets a small number of free sessions when they sign up; after those are used, a plan is needed.

## Troubleshooting

* "Skip to content" is an accessibility link that appears when you press Tab; it is not an error.
* If you are signed in but see **Sign in** on a public page, reload the page; the header checks your session when it loads.
