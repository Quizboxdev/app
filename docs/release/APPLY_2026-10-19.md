### Going live with payments (owner, about 15 minutes, after Paystack approves your business account)
1. Paystack dashboard: copy the **secret key** (Settings, API Keys and Webhooks). In Vercel, project settings, Environment Variables, add `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY` for Production and redeploy.
2. In the same Paystack page set the **webhook URL** to `https://www.quizbox.io/api/payments/webhook/paystack`.
3. In QuizBox: **Admin, Access and Subscriptions, Online payments**: enter the price of one Coin in cedis (the plans cost 10 and 25 Coins, so GHS 1.00 per Coin means GHS 10 a month and GHS 25 a term), click **Save price**, then **Switch payments on**.
4. Make one real test payment yourself with a small amount and confirm the subscription appears. Refund it in Paystack and confirm it is revoked.
5. Set the free allowance on the same screen when you are ready for the gate.

Until steps 1 to 3 are done nothing changes for learners: the Subscribe page keeps showing "Price to be announced" and the contact route.
